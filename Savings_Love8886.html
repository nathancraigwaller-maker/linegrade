How this was measured

Picks are collected from the public record and settled independently against real results — we never take a posted record at face value. A pick counts as verified only if we logged it before the match was played; boards often settle results weeks late, so a pick sitting unresolved when we find it is not proof we were early. Those are counted as claimed. z is how many standard deviations the record sits above what the posted odds already implied — the bar a pick has to clear to be skill rather than favourites winning. A record becomes an edge at z ≥ 2 over 30–50 verified picks.

Not measured here: closing-line value. Grading whether the market moved toward a pick after it was called requires a closing price from a book that actually prices these games, and that data is being collected from today forward. Nothing on this page claims to be CLV.
